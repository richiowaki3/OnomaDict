## 1. Research Overview & Literature Map

### Theme A: Laban Effort Critique & Reliability
- **Bernardet, Fdili Alaoui, Studd, Bradley, Pasquier, Schiphorst (2019)**: "Assessing the reliability of the Laban Movement Analysis system", *PLoS ONE* 14(6):e0218179. Comprehensive empirical validation of LMA by Certified Movement Analysts (CMAs). Krippendorff's $\alpha$ shows lower inter-rater reliability than traditionally assumed.

### Theme B: Application to Expressive Synthesis
- **Nakata et al. (2001)**: Quantitative correlation between robot movement impression and Laban features (*Journal of Robotics Society of Japan*).
- **Chi, Costa, Zhao, Badler (2000)**: "EMOTE", *SIGGRAPH*. Parameterization of Weight, Time, Space for synthetic animation.

### Theme C: Physical Quantification
- **Samadani et al.**: Mapping Weight, Time, Space, Flow to position, velocity, acceleration, jerk, curvature.

### Theme D: Alternative Dimensional Models
- **de Meijer (1989)**: Direction, force, speed, straightness (*J. Nonverbal Behavior*).
- **Montepare et al. (1999)**: Direction, force, tempo, shape.

### Theme E: Mechanistic Reformulation & Visualizing Movement Qualities
- **Fdili Alaoui, Caramiaux, Serrano, Bevilacqua (2012)**: "Movement Qualities as Interaction Modality", *DIS '12*, 761–769. Formulates movement quality as *how motion is executed*, independent of spatial trajectories.
- **Fdili Alaoui, Bevilacqua, Bermudez, Jacquemin (2013)**: "Dance interaction with physical model visuals based on movement qualities", *Int. J. Arts and Tech.* 6(4):357–387. Visualizes Effort via mass-spring models.
- **Fdili Alaoui, Bevilacqua, Jacquemin (2015)**: "Interactive Visuals as Metaphors for Dance Movement Qualities", *ACM TiiS* 5(3), Art.13. Metaphorical visualization of movement qualities via physical simulation.
- **Fdili Alaoui, Françoise, Schiphorst, Studd, Bevilacqua (2017)**: "Seeing, Sensing and Recognizing Laban Movement Qualities", *CHI '17*. Computational recognition of Laban Movement qualities connected to CMA expertise.

## 2. Epistemological Layering (Naïve Physics vs. Real Physics)

- **Naïve Physics (Subjective Cognitive Model)**: Category A (Laban Effort). Observer-dependent model of perceived kinetic energy, force, space, and flow.
- **Real Physics (Objective Fluid Dynamics & Acoustics)**: Category B (Sound Physics) & Category C (Reynolds number of oral airflow).
- **Architecture**: The dictionary separates these layers so that sensory, acoustic, and fluid mechanics axes interact without conflating subjective movement perception with raw physical measurements.
